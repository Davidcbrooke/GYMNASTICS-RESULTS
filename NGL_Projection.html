10-year P&L

Licences grow by the yearly growth rate until they reach the most clubs expected times the share of clubs running each pathway. One club can hold licences in several pathways. Schools and Pre-School gymnasts buy one book for their time (new gymnasts each year = growth plus replacing leavers); Recreational gymnasts buy one every 4 terms. Postage is charged to clubs on each supply order; the cost of posting is a running cost. Pre-School Lesson Plan Packs and the Seasonal Pack are not included. NGL is not VAT registered.
