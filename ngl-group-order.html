Group purchasing · Quarterly order

The more clubs order, the less everyone pays

Standard NGL staff kit with your club's logo: a t-shirt and a quarter zip. Every club's order is added together, and when the total passes each band, the price comes down for everyone in that order.

Live order board

Each product has its own bands. The final price is fixed when the order closes, and every club pays the same price.
