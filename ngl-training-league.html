The Training League

Boost training. Focus, results and fun, every session. Nine conditioning challenges for strength, speed, power, flexibility, balance and control. Coaches film them in training and send them to NGL, and every valid result goes straight into the live league table and onto the gymnast's NGL Profile Card.

Levels 1–4Ages 6–8 · 9–11 · 12–14 · 15+2–5 hrs · 6–10 hrsOpen all season

Why clubs use the Training League

Conditioning that boys actually want to do, with a reason to give their best every session.

🎯Boost training productivityEvery challenge has a clear target, so sessions have focus and gymnasts can see their results improve.
🏁Harness the power of competitionGymnasts compete against boys at the same level, age and training hours across the country.
😄Make training funConditioning becomes a game: beat your best, climb the table, raise your rating.
⚽A format boys recogniseLeague tables, ratings and profile cards, just like the sports games and cards they already love. Engagement every session.
📱Easy to film and sendOne phone, side-on, one continuous take. Email the videos and NGL does the rest.
📊Live league table & Profile CardsEvery result updates the table and the gymnast's NGL Profile Card, and you can compare two gymnasts side by side.
