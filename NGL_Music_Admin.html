Space plays the selected gymnast. Absent gymnasts are skipped. Drag rows to change the order, or use the rotation box to move someone. While music is playing, Space does nothing, so a stray press can't stop a routine: use F to fade out, or K to pause and resume. When a track finishes, the next gymnast is selected but doesn't start until you press Space.
