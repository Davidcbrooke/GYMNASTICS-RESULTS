Parents and coaches told us again how relaxed it felt compared with other competitions. Our new connected music player ran the whole day, and gymnasts were judged consistently all season by the same National judge. That's the standard we're setting for 2027: more gymnasts competing, in competitions that finish on time.

2. Reserve your 2027 places

Demand for 2027 is already high, and many competitions are fully reserved. Reserve your club's places now in the NGL Club Hub. Booking opens on MyBG four months before each competition, and we'll email you the link.

Competition full? Join the waiting list. Not every club takes up all of its reserved places. When the booking deadline passes, any places that haven't been bought go straight to clubs on the waiting list, so joining it gets places to the gymnasts who need them much faster.

3. The General Skills Book: out now, six months early

So many clubs have now registered on the NGL Club Hub that we've brought the General Skills Book forward from April 2027. It's printed and ready to post.

Your 2027 General League licence fee is waived because your club is on the Club Hub. Books are £4 each to your club (RRP £8 if you choose to sell them on). One book covers Levels 1–4.

Aligned to the NGL competitions. The skills in each level match what gymnasts compete at that level.
The right level to compete. Gymnasts can see which level to enter from the skills they can already do.
Clear for parents. Parents can see the level their child is at and how they're progressing.
Focus for coaches. Development areas are set out openly, so gymnast, parent and coach all see the same picture.
Bronze, Silver and Gold execution. Being able to do a skill is different from doing it perfectly 10 times out of 10, so it's easier to decide when a gymnast is ready to compete.
Working With Parents In Sport. A section from our key partner backs our holistic approach to competitive sport, centred on the gymnast and the parent.
