National Finals 2026: the results
Sunday 22 November · Redbourn Leisure Centre
 

Hello everyone,

Thank you for making the first NGL National Finals such a huge success. The atmosphere was incredible, your coaches smashed the timings, and every gymnast on that floor had earned their place.

The full results for both sessions are below. Congratulations to every gymnast, coach and club.

I've also written up what went well and what we're changing for 2027. It's linked at the bottom if you'd like to read it.

Please share this with all your parents, including the look back at what worked and what didn't. Everyone should be incredibly proud of what their children have taken part in. As an organisation, we want to be open and transparent with everyone, listen to feedback and make changes where they're needed.
