The challenges

Filmed in training: fixed camera side-on, whole body in shot, one continuous take, a tape measure or line in shot for distances, and the coach says the gymnast's name and the challenge at the start. Up to 2 attempts per challenge each time it's filmed; the best valid attempt counts towards the season.
