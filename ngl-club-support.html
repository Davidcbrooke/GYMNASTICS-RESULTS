Strong foundations that reduce pressure and risk

Operating a gymnastics club is complex. Clubs are often forced to build systems in isolation, duplicating effort and learning through trial and error.

NGL's Operate Better pillar focuses on reducing that burden by encouraging shared systems, common approaches and proven best practice.

Financial models, pricing structures and budgeting tools

Support could include

  • Shared pricing frameworks aligned to training hours and pathways
  • Budgeting templates tailored to gymnastics club operations
  • Cashflow planning tools that reflect seasonal income patterns

Benefits of shared approaches

  • Reduces guesswork around pricing and sustainability
  • Helps clubs benchmark against similar organisations
  • Supports more consistent and transparent communication with parents
  • Improves long-term financial planning and resilience
Outcome: Clubs make informed decisions with greater confidence, rather than reacting to short-term pressures.

Support could include

  • Guidance on selecting and integrating class management systems
  • Shared approaches to competition scoring and data handling
  • Consistent data structures across training, competition and finance

Benefits of shared systems

  • Less duplication of data entry and admin
  • Improved data accuracy and reliability
  • Easier onboarding for staff and volunteers moving between clubs
  • Clearer insight into participation, progression and retention
Outcome: Clubs spend less time managing systems and more time delivering quality experiences.

Our coaches are OUR business

We have to make coaches feel valued again. They need to be paid better, trained better, treated better and supported better.

Great clubs are built by great coaches. The Coach Better pillar focuses on strengthening the coaching workforce by making development more accessible, more connected and more sustainable — while ensuring high standards are maintained across the sport.

Rather than clubs working in isolation, NGL supports shared approaches that help knowledge, opportunity and expertise flow more easily across the coaching community.

Internal training programmes

We believe in keeping things simple, promoting the transfer of skills within clubs.

Support could include

  • Structured internal CPD frameworks for clubs
  • Shared models for mentoring, shadowing and peer learning
  • Clear pathways for assistant coaches to progress

Benefits of shared approaches

  • Reduces reliance on external courses alone
  • Ensures knowledge stays within the club
  • Accelerates development of less experienced coaches
  • Builds stronger, more consistent coaching teams
Outcome: Clubs develop confident coaches from within, creating continuity and stability.

British Gymnastics training programmes

Making qualifications accessible without lowering standards.

Support could include

  • Better signposting and alignment of BG qualifications to club needs
  • Support with planning, hosting and accessing courses
  • Encouraging flexible delivery models where possible

Benefits of shared planning and access

  • Removes unnecessary barriers to qualification
  • Improves uptake and retention of coaches
  • Helps clubs plan workforce development strategically
  • Ensures qualifications remain high quality and relevant
Outcome: A larger, better-qualified coaching workforce across the sport.

Coach Better Partner

Elevate Volunteering

Who are we?

My name is Gemma Wright and I am the founder of Elevate Volunteering. I have been a gymnastics coach since 2008, from volunteering to a full time career. I have been lucky enough to have a career in the sport I am so passionate about, working in a number of clubs in the East Region where I have been Recreational Manager, Competitive Coach, Events Coordinator, National Judge and Volunteer Leader. With Elevate I am looking forward to showing my love for the sport and sharing my knowledge and experiences with the next generation of coaches.

What we will do

Elevate Volunteering will work with your club to provide introductory courses to ensure a positive start to your coaching career, including:

  • Introduction to Health and Safety / Wellbeing — one of the most important aspects of coaching: how to deal with situations, who to go to when in need, and how to create safe set-ups in the settings your club provides.
  • Introduction to Recreational Gymnastics — preparations and safe set-ups for all skills required for progressive gymnastics, using set-ups that have been used in a number of clubs and are easy to transfer.
  • Introduction to Judging — the flip side to coaching: the basics of officiating a gymnastics competition, the roles a judge plays, and an introduction to the judging process.

After the courses, Young Leaders are allocated a mentor from the Senior Leadership Team and attend a minimum of 2 hours each week working hands-on in a class, usually with their mentor. Each term they attend a YLVP review meeting to discuss what they have learnt and outline a plan for the next term.

People first. Always

Gymnastics clubs are people-centred environments. Gymnasts, parents, coaches and volunteers all need to feel safe, supported and listened to. The Care Better pillar focuses on creating consistent, safeguarding-led cultures where welfare is embedded into everyday practice — not treated as a compliance exercise.

By sharing best practice and aligned approaches, clubs can reduce risk, improve experiences and build long-term trust with families.

Human Resources (HR)

Supporting the people who deliver the sport.

Support could include

  • HR frameworks appropriate for small and medium-sized clubs
  • Contract templates and role clarity for employed and freelance staff
  • Guidance on performance management, support and progression
  • Wellbeing and workload considerations for coaches and staff

Benefits of shared HR approaches

  • Reduces risk and uncertainty for club owners
  • Creates clarity and fairness for staff
  • Supports retention by improving working conditions
  • Moves clubs away from informal or reactive people management
Outcome: Staff feel supported, valued and clear about expectations — leading to more stable teams.

Safeguarding

Embedding safety into culture, not just paperwork. Making it easier to understand the excellent support provided by British Gymnastics.

Support could include

  • Clear safeguarding frameworks aligned across clubs
  • Consistent procedures for reporting, escalation and response
  • Training and education beyond minimum compliance
  • Shared learning from incidents and best practice

Benefits of a shared safeguarding approach

  • Reduces inconsistency and confusion
  • Builds confidence in how concerns are handled
  • Supports staff and volunteers in difficult situations
  • Reassures parents that welfare is taken seriously everywhere
Outcome: Safer environments where everyone understands their role in protecting gymnasts.

Parent & Gymnast Support

Improving the experience beyond the gym floor.

Support could include

  • Best practice in parent communication and expectation management
  • Membership benefits and rewards
  • Clear guidance on competition processes and pathways
  • Consistent messaging around development, not just results
  • Tools and templates for handling queries, concerns and feedback

Benefits of shared customer experience standards

  • Reduces conflict and misunderstanding
  • Builds trust and transparency with families
  • Supports calmer, more positive competition environments
  • Improves retention and long-term engagement
Outcome: Parents feel informed and reassured; gymnasts feel supported and understood.

Culture & Environment

The invisible factor that shapes everything.

Support could include

  • Guidance on creating positive, athlete-centred cultures
  • Shared principles for competition and training environments
  • Encouraging language, behaviour and expectations across clubs
  • Education on psychological safety and wellbeing

Benefits of aligned cultural standards

  • Reduces harmful practices and pressure
  • Encourages enjoyment alongside ambition
  • Supports long-term athlete development
  • Protects the reputation of clubs and the sport
Outcome: Healthy environments where people want to stay and grow.

Building sustainable, future-ready clubs

For clubs to thrive long term, growth must be planned, informed and sustainable. The Grow Better pillar focuses on helping clubs make smarter decisions around sales, marketing and programme development — using data, insight and shared national visibility rather than guesswork.

By aligning approaches and sharing intelligence, clubs can grow in a way that protects quality, culture and capacity.

Sales

Turning demand into sustainable membership.

Support could include

  • Proven sales and conversion frameworks for enquiries and trials
  • Clear pricing and package structures aligned to pathways
  • Tools to support retention and upselling responsibly
  • Guidance on capacity management and waiting lists

Benefits of shared sales approaches

  • Improves conversion rates without aggressive selling
  • Creates clearer, more consistent parent messaging
  • Helps clubs manage demand without overloading staff or facilities
  • Supports predictable income and planning
Outcome: Clubs convert interest into long-term, sustainable participation.

Data & Insight

Understanding the industry and your club.

Support could include

  • Access to anonymised industry-level data and trends
  • Club-level insight on participation, retention and progression
  • Benchmarking against similar clubs or regions
  • Using competition and training data to inform decisions

Benefits of shared data and insight

  • Moves decision-making from instinct to evidence
  • Helps clubs identify risks and opportunities early
  • Supports better forecasting and investment decisions
  • Strengthens conversations with partners, facilities and funders
Outcome: Clubs make confident, informed decisions based on real insight.

Social Media & Digital Presence

Visibility without overwhelm.

Support could include

  • Simple, repeatable content frameworks for clubs
  • Guidance on safeguarding-aware digital practice
  • Showcasing development, progress and positive stories
  • Reducing pressure to "perform" on social platforms

Benefits of aligned digital approaches

  • Saves time and reduces content burnout
  • Creates more authentic, consistent messaging
  • Improves brand trust with parents and communities
  • Supports recruitment and retention naturally
Outcome: Clubs maintain a healthy, effective digital presence without excessive effort.

National Marketing Campaigns

Raising visibility for the whole sport.

Support could include

  • Coordinated national campaigns clubs can plug into locally
  • Shared messaging that promotes gymnastics positively
  • Campaigns aligned to recruitment, retention and awareness
  • Reducing the need for clubs to create everything from scratch

Benefits of national-level campaigns

  • Greater reach than individual club efforts
  • Consistent, professional messaging across the country
  • Reduced marketing burden on clubs
  • Stronger public perception of gymnastics as a whole
Outcome: Increased demand, improved retention and a stronger national profile for gymnastics clubs.

Overall Impact of Growing Better Together

When clubs grow using shared insight and coordinated approaches:

  • Growth becomes manageable, not chaotic
  • Quality is protected alongside expansion
  • Clubs are better positioned to invest in people and facilities
  • The sport presents a more unified, professional image
Outcome: Clubs that grow with confidence, clarity and sustainability — supported by data, visibility and shared national momentum.

Let's talk!

Gymnastics clubs are often isolated — operating independently, solving the same problems repeatedly, and relying on informal networks for support. The Connect Better pillar focuses on bringing clubs, people and organisations together in a more structured, purposeful way.

By improving connection across the sport, NGL helps clubs learn faster, feel supported, and access opportunities that would be difficult to achieve alone.

Inter-club collaboration

Working together, not in isolation.

Support could include

  • Creating opportunities for clubs to collaborate regionally and nationally
  • Encouraging shared problem-solving across similar clubs
  • Facilitating conversations around common challenges and solutions

Benefits of stronger inter-club collaboration

  • Reduces duplication of effort
  • Helps clubs learn from real-world experience
  • Builds trust and mutual understanding
  • Encourages a healthier, more supportive competitive environment
Outcome: Clubs feel less isolated and more confident knowing support exists beyond their own walls.

Shared learning & best practice

Turning experience into collective knowledge.

Support could include

  • Sharing proven approaches, templates and case studies
  • Learning from what works — and what doesn't — across the network
  • Providing spaces for discussion, reflection and improvement

Benefits of shared learning

  • Accelerates improvement across clubs
  • Avoids repeating common mistakes
  • Raises standards without imposing rules
  • Helps good practice spread naturally
Outcome: Clubs improve faster by learning from each other's experience.

Partner ecosystem

Connecting clubs to trusted expertise.

Support could include

  • Introducing clubs to aligned partners and service providers
  • Creating clarity around where external expertise can add value
  • Encouraging partnerships that support clubs across the five pillars

Benefits of a connected partner ecosystem

  • Reduces time spent searching for solutions
  • Improves confidence in external services
  • Aligns providers with real club needs
  • Supports consistency and quality across the sport
Outcome: Clubs gain easier access to the right support, at the right time.

Community & culture

Building a sense of belonging.

Support could include

  • Creating opportunities for informal connection and networking
  • Encouraging positive, values-led interaction across the sport
  • Reinforcing the idea that progress is collective, not competitive

Benefits of stronger community connection

  • Improves morale and wellbeing
  • Encourages openness and trust
  • Supports long-term engagement with the sport
  • Strengthens the overall reputation of gymnastics
Outcome: A more connected, collaborative gymnastics community.
