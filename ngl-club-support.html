5 Pillars to Make Clubs Better

Every club faces the same challenges — but very few get to solve them together. The National Gymnastics League pools the collective experience and scale of the whole network so every club can run more efficiently as a business, not just as a training environment. That means standardising things every club already needs — coaching expertise, IT systems, HR advice and more — instead of each club sourcing and negotiating them alone, for a more consistent, higher-quality result and real savings in time and money.

⚙️ Operate Better ▾

Strong foundations that reduce pressure and risk. Operating a gymnastics club is complex. Clubs are often forced to build systems in isolation, duplicating effort and learning through trial and error. NGL's Operate Better pillar focuses on reducing that burden by encouraging shared systems, common approaches and proven best practice.

🎓 Coach Better ▾

Our coaches are OUR business. Great clubs are built by great coaches. The Coach Better pillar focuses on strengthening the coaching workforce by making development more accessible, more connected and more sustainable, so knowledge, opportunity and expertise can flow more easily across the coaching community.

🛡️ Care Better ▾

People first. Always. Gymnasts, parents, coaches and volunteers all need to feel safe, supported and listened to. The Care Better pillar focuses on consistent, safeguarding-led cultures where welfare is embedded into everyday practice, reducing risk and building long-term trust with families.

📈 Grow Better ▾

Building sustainable, future-ready clubs. For clubs to thrive long term, growth must be planned, informed and sustainable. The Grow Better pillar focuses on helping clubs make smarter decisions around sales, marketing and programme development, using data and shared national visibility rather than guesswork.

🤝 Connect Better ▾

Let's talk! Gymnastics clubs are often isolated, solving the same problems repeatedly and relying on informal networks for support. The Connect Better pillar focuses on bringing clubs, people and organisations together in a more structured, purposeful way, so clubs learn faster and access opportunities they couldn't reach alone.

Coach Better Partner

Elevate Volunteering

Who are we?

My name is Gemma Wright and I am the founder of Elevate Volunteering. I have been a gymnastics coach since 2008, from volunteering to a full time career. I have been lucky enough to have a career in the sport I am so passionate about, working in a number of clubs in the East Region where I have been Recreational Manager, Competitive Coach, Events Coordinator, National Judge and Volunteer Leader. With Elevate I am looking forward to showing my love for the sport and sharing my knowledge and experiences with the next generation of coaches.

What we will do

Elevate Volunteering will work with your club to provide introductory courses to ensure a positive start to your coaching career, including:

  • Introduction to Health and Safety / Wellbeing — one of the most important aspects of coaching: how to deal with situations, who to go to when in need, and how to create safe set-ups in the settings your club provides.
  • Introduction to Recreational Gymnastics — preparations and safe set-ups for all skills required for progressive gymnastics, using set-ups that have been used in a number of clubs and are easy to transfer.
  • Introduction to Judging — the flip side to coaching: the basics of officiating a gymnastics competition, the roles a judge plays, and an introduction to the judging process.

After the courses, Young Leaders are allocated a mentor from the Senior Leadership Team and attend a minimum of 2 hours each week working hands-on in a class, usually with their mentor. Each term they attend a YLVP review meeting to discuss what they have learnt and outline a plan for the next term.
